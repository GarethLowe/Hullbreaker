HULLBREAK

HEAVY CRUISER · MERIDIAN ACTUAL

Your ship is not a health bar. It is fourteen pressurised compartments holding seventy-odd components, wired together by three networks — power, data and coolant — and crewed by four hundred and twenty hands in eight divisions who have to walk to a problem before they can fix it. Hold W to build speed; let go and the ship brakes itself back to a stop.

So does everything you are shooting at — and you can read its interior in the same detail as your own, in the panel on the right. You do not have to destroy a turret: cutting the conduit that feeds it, boiling the loop that cools it, or killing the gunnery division that lays it will all do. These are capital ships fighting at four to eight kilometres, so an engagement runs for minutes. Watch what your shooting actually breaks.
